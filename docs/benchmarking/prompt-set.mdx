`prompts/dev_v1.jsonl` has 30 prompts — 10 `LOW`, 10 `MID`, 10 `HIGH` — across ten task types: Q&A, features, debugging, tests, DevOps, refactoring, review, architecture, security, and docs.

## Difficulty rubric

Each prompt is scored 0–2 on five dimensions: reasoning depth, scope, context, risk, and specialised knowledge.

| Total score | Tier |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | `LOW` |
| 4–7 | `MID` |
| 8–10 | `HIGH` |

The runner validates labels against the rubric on load.

<Warning>
  The set is **versioned benchmark data**. Once results are published, `dev_v1` is frozen and changes go into `dev_v2`.
</Warning>
