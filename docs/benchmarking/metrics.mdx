---
title: "Metrics"
description: "What the benchmark measures and how to interpret it."
---

- **Rubric agreement** — did the router pick the tier the rubric expected? (Not called "accuracy": expected tiers are human labels, not ground truth.)
- **Under-routing** — hard task → weak model (quality risk).
- **Over-routing** — easy task → strong model (cost waste).
- **Decision latency** — mean / median / p95 of the routing call only.
- **Stability** — same answer across repeats?
- **Per-category breakdown** and **failure rate** — timeouts, parse errors, and unknown models are reported separately, never hidden.

## Interpreting results

There is no single "winner" metric. Under-routing costs quality, over-routing costs money — which matters more depends on you. A router's tier distribution is its *fingerprint*, not a score.
