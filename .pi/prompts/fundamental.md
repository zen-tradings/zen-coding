Fundamental analysis: $@

Rules for this task:
- Every quantitative claim must cite its source (filing, dataset, computed value)
- Distinguish clearly between reported facts, your calculations, and your inferences
- If data is unavailable or stale, say so — do not estimate silently

Structure:
1. **Business & moat** — what they sell, to whom, unit economics if available
2. **Financial quality** — revenue/margin trends, accruals vs. cash flow
   divergence, balance-sheet risks, one-time items buried in "adjusted" figures
3. **Valuation** — multiples vs. peers and vs. own history; state what growth/
   margin assumptions the current price implies
4. **Risks** — the 3 most credible ways this investment loses money, with the
   evidence for each
5. **What would change your mind** — the 2–3 observable metrics to monitor

End with a one-paragraph verdict that a PM could act on, including conviction
level and why.
